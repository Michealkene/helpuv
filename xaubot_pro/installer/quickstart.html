Requirements

Installation

1 Run the Installer

Right-click install.bat and select "Run as administrator". The installer will automatically set up Python, dependencies, and create desktop shortcuts.

2 Complete the Setup Wizard

The installer will launch a setup wizard. Enter your MetaTrader 5 credentials:

  • MT5 Login: Your account number
  • MT5 Password: Your trading password
  • MT5 Server: Your broker's server name (e.g., "ICMarkets-MT5")
  • Risk Per Trade: How much you want to risk per trade in USD
3 Open MetaTrader 5

Make sure MetaTrader 5 is running and logged into your account. The bot needs MT5 to be open to place trades.

4 Launch the Dashboard

Double-click the XAUBOT Pro Dashboard shortcut on your desktop. The dashboard will open in your web browser.

5 Start Trading

In the dashboard, go to the Live Trading page and click "Start Bot". The bot will begin monitoring for setups automatically.

Troubleshooting

"MT5 connection failed"

  • Make sure MetaTrader 5 is open and logged in
  • Check that your credentials in Settings are correct
  • Try restarting MetaTrader 5
  • Ensure "Algo Trading" is enabled in MT5 (Tools > Options > Expert Advisors)

"Bot not placing trades"

  • The bot only trades during the Asian session (roughly 00:00-04:00 GMT)
  • Not every day produces a valid setup - this is normal
  • Check the dashboard logs for detailed status information

"Dashboard won't open"

  • Make sure no other application is using port 8501
  • Try running the shortcut as administrator
  • Check that the virtual environment was created properly during installation

Updating Your Settings

You can change your trading settings at any time through the Dashboard's Settings page. Changes are saved to config.json and take effect on the next trade cycle.

To re-run the setup wizard, open a command prompt and run:
