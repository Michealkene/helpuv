## 6. Important Notes and Warnings

### Regulatory Landscape
- **Prop firms are NOT regulated** as financial institutions in most jurisdictions. Your funds are at risk if a firm shuts down.
- **Retail brokers** operating under FCA/ASIC/CySEC provide investor protection (up to $85K FCA, $500K SIPC for US, $20K CySEC).
- Under **EU/UK/AU regulation**, gold leverage is capped at **1:20** regardless of what the broker advertises. Higher leverage is only available under offshore (non-EU) entities.

### Gold Trading Specifics
- **1 standard lot of XAUUSD** = 100 troy ounces. At $2,000/oz, 1 lot = $200,000 notional value.
- **Pip value:** 1 pip ($0.01 move) on 1 lot = $1.00. But gold moves in dollars, not pips -- a $10 move = $1,000 per lot.
- **Average daily range:** Gold typically moves $20-$40+ per day, making it one of the most volatile instruments.
- **Swap rates:** Holding gold overnight incurs swap charges. Short gold swaps are typically positive (you earn), long gold swaps are negative (you pay).
- **Correlated instruments:** DXY (inverse), USDJPY (inverse), Silver (positive), S&P 500 (variable), US 10Y yields (inverse).

### Risk Management for Gold
- **Position sizing:** For a $100,000 prop firm account with 5% daily loss limit ($5,000), a 1-lot gold position risks $1,000 per $10 move. Maximum recommended position: 2-3 lots with a 15-20 point stop loss.
- **News events to watch:** FOMC, NFP, CPI, GDP, geopolitical tensions, USD strength
- **Best trading sessions for gold:** London (07:00-16:00 GMT) and New York (12:00-21:00 GMT) overlap (12:00-16:00 GMT) has the highest volume.
